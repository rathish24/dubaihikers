# Product Vision

## Purpose

Dubai Hikers helps hikers discover and book well-organised guided hiking events across the UAE.

## Problem

Trail information is fragmented, difficulty is often unclear, and booking local guided hikes commonly relies on unstructured messages. Hikers need a trustworthy place to understand an event before committing.

## Vision

Create the most dependable digital starting point for guided mountain experiences in RAK: local knowledge, honest grading, small groups, clear preparation, and simple booking.

## Audience

- UAE residents looking for weekend outdoor experiences
- Solo hikers who want the confidence of a group
- Visitors seeking locally guided UAE mountain hikes
- Experienced hikers looking for advanced routes
- Companies and private groups arranging outdoor events

## Principles

1. Safety information is part of the product, not fine print.
2. Difficulty and preparation requirements must be honest.
3. Event details should be understandable at a glance.
4. Booking should require as few steps as possible.
5. Mobile use is the primary experience.
6. Trail and customer data must have a clear source of truth.

## Success Measures

- Event-detail to checkout conversion
- Completed paid bookings
- Repeat hikers
- Low cancellation and refund confusion
- Safety acknowledgement completion
- Positive post-hike feedback
